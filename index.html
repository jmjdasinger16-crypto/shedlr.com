Questions

Straight answers before you sign up.

What exactly does $199 cover?

Your first full month of the Shedlr Growth Plan: hand-verified exclusive leads in your category and service area, your industry-tailored SEO program, and full access to the client portal. each month, the plan renews at $199 per month.

Are there per-lead charges on top of the subscription?

No. The monthly plan is the price. We priced the first month low on purpose so you can generate revenue from our work before the standard subscription rate begins.

Are leads ever shared with my competitors?

Never. Each verified lead is assigned to exactly one business. We do not resell, syndicate or recycle leads to other buyers in your area.

How fast do leads start arriving?

Most accounts see their first verified lead within 24 to 48 hours of activation, once we have confirmed your service area and category criteria.

What happens if a booked appointment does not show?

No-shows on booked appointments are credited back to your account automatically. You should not pay for someone who did not turn up.

How is the SEO work tailored to my business type?

We start from the search behavior of your industry — the repair, claim, emergency and comparison phrases buyers in your trade use — then build location pages and profile work around the ZIP codes you actually serve.

Is there a contract?

No. The plan is month-to-month and you can cancel from the client portal at any time. There is no cancellation fee.

Do I have to sit through a sales call?

No. Sign up online and our team gets to work. If you would rather talk it through first, call (806) 460-9488 during business hours.

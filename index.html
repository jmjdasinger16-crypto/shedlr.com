Questions, answered

Everything you need to know.

Straightforward pricing, straightforward service. Here are the answers to the most common questions.

How much do leads cost?

Pricing is simple and varies by category and lead type. Fill out the order form and we will confirm your rate before you pay anything.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no trial period. You purchase lead credits upfront and receive leads as they are verified.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation requires 30 days' advance notice as described in the Shedlr Terms of Service.

What does "pre-qualified" mean?

Every lead is manually researched and screened by our team before it reaches you. We confirm real contact information, genuine interest, and category-specific criteria — like documented hail damage for roofing claims — so you only receive leads who are ready to talk.

How are leads verified?

We manually research and pre-qualify each lead before delivering it to you. No automated scraping — real people, real prospecting.

Do you check my service area?

When you submit an order, we check availability for your ZIP code. We are actively expanding coverage nationwide.

What types of leads are available?

Roofing, auto hail damage, home services (HVAC, plumbing, pest control), solar, real estate, personal training, life coaching, maintenance, dog walking, house cleaning, landscaping, tutoring, photography, handyman services, moving, catering, event planning, home insurance, and vehicle insurance — delivered as booked appointments, scheduled calls, or raw leads.

What business scaling services does Shedlr offer?

Website design, virtual answering support, and SEO can be selected individually or combined with Shedlr lead generation as a broader business scaling solution.

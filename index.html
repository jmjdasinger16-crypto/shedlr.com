Questions before you buy

Frequently Asked Questions

Shedlr is designed to be simple, transparent, and affordable.

How much do leads cost?

Lead prices vary by category, ranging from $1 to $10 per lead. Check the category grid or pricing calculator for current rates. You purchase lead credits in the quantity you choose, and we deliver verified leads until your credits are fulfilled.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no trial period. You purchase lead credits upfront and receive leads as they are verified.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation requires 30 days' advance notice as described in the Shedlr Terms of Service. Any unused lead credits remain available in your account.

How are leads verified?

We manually research and verify each lead before delivering it to you. No automated scraping — real people, real leads.

What types of leads are available?

Personal training, life coaching, maintenance, dog walking, house cleaning, landscaping, tutoring, photography, handyman services, moving, catering, and event planning.

Do you guarantee lead quality or conversion?

No. We verify that each lead is a real person with relevant interest, but we cannot guarantee any specific outcome, conversion rate, or revenue result. Progress depends on your outreach, pricing, and business practices.

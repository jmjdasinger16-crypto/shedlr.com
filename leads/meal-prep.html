Questions, answered

Meal prep leads, explained.

What do you verify for meal prep leads?

We manually confirm genuine interest in purchasing prepared meals, basic service-area fit, timing, valid contact information, and relevant meal preferences when available.

Can I focus on weekly subscription customers?

Yes. Tell us that recurring weekly plans are your priority and we can focus prospecting on that type of customer when available in your market.

Can you target fitness or macro meal customers?

Yes. Meal-prep prospecting can be focused around fitness, high-protein, calorie-conscious, macro-friendly, family meal, or other offers that match your business.

How are meal prep leads delivered?

You choose: a booked appointment, a scheduled phone call, or a raw lead with verified contact and interest details.

Do you check my service area?

Yes. When you submit an order, we check availability for your ZIP code and can target customers around the delivery or pickup area you serve.

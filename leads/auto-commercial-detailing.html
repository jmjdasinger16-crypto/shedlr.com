What kinds of detailing businesses can use Shedlr?

Mobile detailers, auto detailing shops, interior and exterior detailers, fleet-detailing providers, dealership vendors, and commercial detailing businesses can all use Shedlr.

Can you help me target commercial or fleet accounts?

Yes. Tell us in the signup form that you want fleet, dealership, business, or other recurring commercial detailing opportunities and which market you serve.

Can you help book appointments for me?

Yes. Select Appointment Booking and tell us how you prefer detailing appointments, estimates, or consultations to be scheduled.

Can I target only my local service area?

Yes. Enter your service ZIP code and include any radius, city, or territory preferences in the form.

Can Shedlr also help with my website or phones?

Yes. The signup form includes optional website design, virtual answering, SEO, and a full business scaling solution.

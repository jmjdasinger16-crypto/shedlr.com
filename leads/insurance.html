Independent agents, local agencies, brokerages, and insurance sales teams can use Shedlr to generate and qualify prospective customers.

Yes. Tell us whether you want home, auto, life, commercial, bundled-policy, or another type of insurance prospect and we can tailor the campaign around your target market.

Yes. Enter your service ZIP code and include the states, cities, radius, or territory where your agency is licensed and wants to receive opportunities.

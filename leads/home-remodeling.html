Qualification criteria

Our home remodeling verification checklist.

Before a home remodeling lead reaches your portal, our team confirms every item below. If it does not clear the checklist, it is not delivered.

  • Project scope defined — kitchen, bath, addition, flooring or whole-home
  • Realistic budget range confirmed and above your job minimum
  • Property ownership and both decision-makers verified
  • Financing plan noted — cash, HELOC, contractor financing or insurance
  • Desired start window stated and service ZIP matched to your coverage

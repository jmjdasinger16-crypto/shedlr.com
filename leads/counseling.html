Questions, answered

Counseling leads, explained.

What kinds of counseling practices can use Shedlr?

Independent counselors, therapists, group practices, private practices, marriage and family counseling offices, and other eligible counseling providers can use Shedlr to generate prospective-client inquiries.

Does Shedlr perform clinical screening?

No. Shedlr helps with prospecting and general lead qualification. Your licensed practice remains responsible for clinical screening, diagnosis, eligibility, consent, intake, emergencies, and determining whether services are appropriate.

Can you help book consultations?

Yes. Select Appointment Booking and tell us how you prefer introductory consultations or intake calls to be scheduled.

Can I target only areas where I am licensed or accepting clients?

Yes. Enter your service ZIP code and include any city, state, telehealth, licensing, or territory preferences in the form so targeting can be aligned with the areas you want to serve.

Can Shedlr also help with my website or phones?

Yes. The signup form includes optional website design, virtual answering, SEO, and a full business scaling solution.

The grind is the strategy

We don't out-market the competition.
We outwork them.

Craig's belief is simple: talent gets you noticed, but grind gets you paid. Every lead Shedlr ships is manually researched, personally contacted, and hand-verified — because the shortcut every other lead service takes is the exact thing our customers pay us to refuse.

That standard doesn't come from a policy document. It comes from a founder who still answers customer calls on Sundays and treats every client's growth like it's his own.

"I built this company by knocking on doors nobody else would knock on. That's still how we win — one real conversation, one real customer, one real result at a time." — Craig Busby, Founder
The origin

Door to door.
Then dashboard to dashboard.

Before there was a website, a client portal, or a lead-verification workflow, there was just Craig — walking Lubbock, knocking on doors, and connecting small businesses to the customers who actually needed them. No scripts. No cold-call software. No purchased contact lists. Just conversations.

What Craig learned in those early months became the entire foundation of Shedlr: businesses don't need more contacts, they need real ones. Real people. Real interest. Real intent to buy. Everything Shedlr has built since — the pre-qualification workflow, the client portal, the growth services — started with that one door-to-door insight.

Today Shedlr serves businesses across the country in roofing, HVAC, auto services, coaching, home services, and more. But the standard hasn't changed since day one: every lead is a person Craig would knock on a door for himself.

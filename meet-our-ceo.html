Why Shedlr exists

Built from the sales floor, not a slide deck.

Craig Busby built Shedlr after years of watching capable businesses buy the same recycled contact list as their competitors and call it marketing. The problem was never effort. It was that the industry sold volume when operators needed verified, exclusive opportunity.

Shedlr was assembled inside a working sales organization in West Texas. The qualification calls that most platforms automate away are still made by people here — and the standard for what counts as a lead is set by someone who has had to close one.

  • Leads verified by named team members, never by script alone
  • One buyer per lead, without exception
  • Pricing designed so clients profit before full subscription cost
In his words

“If the lead does not close, we did not do our job.”

That is why the first month is $19.99. It gives an owner a full billing cycle to measure real revenue against our work before deciding whether the subscription earns its place in their budget.

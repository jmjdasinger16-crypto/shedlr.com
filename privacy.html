Privacy
Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how LifeRise Unlimited Corporation, doing business as Shedlr, collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Shedlr.com, purchase leads, communicate with us, or access our services.

Please read carefully. By using Shedlr, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service and Disclaimer.

1. Who we are

Shedlr is a lead generation and customer acquisition service operated by LifeRise Unlimited Corporation. We provide businesses with pre-qualified leads, appointment booking, scheduled calls, and related client portal services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, business name, email address, phone number, service category, service area, billing information, account credentials, order details, support requests, communications, and any other information you submit through our website, forms, portal, email, phone, or text messaging.

When you use our website or portal, we may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, timestamps, and similar usage data.

3. Lead and prospect information

As part of providing lead generation services, Shedlr may collect information about prospective customers from publicly available sources, direct inquiries, referrals, advertisements, forms, telephone conversations, text messages, email communications, or other lawful sources. This information may include a prospect's name, contact information, location, expressed service need, scheduling preferences, qualification details, and other information relevant to determining whether the prospect is a suitable lead for a participating business.

We use this information to verify interest, assess lead quality, match leads with appropriate businesses, schedule appointments or calls when requested, prevent fraud or duplicate lead delivery, and administer our services.

4. How we use information

We may use information to operate and improve Shedlr; create and manage accounts; process purchases; deliver leads; schedule calls or appointments; communicate with customers and prospects; provide support; verify identities and contact information; prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity; comply with legal obligations; analyze website and service performance; and develop or improve our products, features, and marketing.

5. Communications

If you provide a phone number or email address, we may use it for service-related communications, including order confirmations, lead delivery notices, appointment coordination, billing notices, account alerts, customer support, and other operational messages. Where permitted by law and with any consent required by applicable law, we may also send promotional communications.

You may opt out of promotional text messages by replying STOP and may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe instructions included in those messages. Opting out of promotional communications does not prevent us from sending necessary transactional or service-related messages.

6. Payment information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment processors such as Stripe. Shedlr generally does not store complete payment card numbers directly on its own servers. Payment processors may collect and process your payment information under their own privacy policies and security practices.

7. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the business, including payment processors, hosting providers, communications providers, analytics services, scheduling services, customer support tools, data storage providers, and other vendors performing services on our behalf.

We may also share qualified lead information with the business customer purchasing or receiving that lead so the business can contact the prospect regarding the requested service. We may disclose information when required by law, legal process, court order, governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Shedlr, our users, our customers, or others.

We may transfer information in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, corporate transaction, or similar business event.

8. No sale of personal information for unrelated purposes

Shedlr does not sell personal information to unrelated third parties for their independent use in ways unrelated to providing the Shedlr lead generation service. Lead information may be provided to a participating business as part of the lead service when the prospect has expressed interest relevant to that business category or service request.

9. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics tools, or similar technologies to keep the site functioning, remember preferences, understand traffic and usage, improve performance, measure marketing effectiveness, and protect against fraud or abuse. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain technologies may affect site functionality.

10. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and support legitimate business operations. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and why it was collected.

11. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your privacy choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional privacy rights. We will honor legally valid requests as required by applicable law.

13. Children's privacy

Shedlr is intended for adults and businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information was collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

14. Third-party websites and services

Shedlr may link to third-party websites, payment processors, scheduling services, social media platforms, or other external services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Shedlr is not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

15. U.S. operations

Shedlr primarily operates in the United States. If you access the service from another jurisdiction, your information may be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your location.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices, technology, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice where appropriate.

17. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to submit a privacy request, contact Shedlr at support@shedlr.com or (307) 303-7530. Please include enough information for us to understand and respond to your request.

Privacy
Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how LifeRise Unlimited Corporation, doing business as Shedlr, collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Shedlr.com or our services.

Please read carefully. By using Shedlr, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Shedlr is a lead generation and customer acquisition service operated by LifeRise Unlimited Corporation.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including name, business name, email address, phone number, service category, service area, billing information, account information, order details, support requests, and communications. We may also collect technical and website usage information.

3. Lead and prospect information

As part of our lead generation services, Shedlr may collect information about prospective customers from lawful sources, direct inquiries, referrals, advertisements, forms, telephone conversations, text messages, and email communications. We use this information to verify interest, assess lead quality, match leads with appropriate businesses, schedule appointments or calls when requested, and administer our services.

4. How we use information

We may use information to operate and improve Shedlr; manage accounts; process purchases; deliver leads; schedule calls or appointments; communicate with customers and prospects; provide support; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with legal obligations; and analyze service performance.

5. SMS and communications privacy

If you expressly opt in to receive SMS messages from LifeRise Unlimited Corporation/Shedlr, we may use your mobile number to send consulting and lead-generation offers, requested follow-ups, and service-related messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent to receive marketing SMS messages is not required as a condition of purchase.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent records will not be shared with third parties for unrelated marketing. We may provide information to vendors that act on our behalf solely to deliver or support our messaging services, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

6. Payment information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment processors such as Stripe. Shedlr generally does not store complete payment card numbers directly on its own servers.

7. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, including payment, hosting, communications, analytics, scheduling, support, and storage providers. We may also share qualified lead information with a business receiving that lead when appropriate to provide the requested lead service. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, property, or security.

8. No sale for unrelated purposes

Shedlr does not sell personal information to unrelated third parties for their independent use in ways unrelated to providing Shedlr services.

9. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics tools, or similar technologies to operate the site, understand usage, improve performance, and protect against fraud or abuse.

10. Data retention and security

We retain personal information as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Your privacy choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

12. Children's privacy

Shedlr is intended for adults and businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

13. Third-party services

Shedlr may use or link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here and the effective date will be updated.

15. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to support@shedlr.com or (307) 303-7530.
